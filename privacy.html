Introduction

Snake Changer respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices for the game Snake Changer. Our game is an offline casual snake game and is designed not to collect any personal information from users.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information:

  • No name, email, phone number
  • No location data (GPS)
  • No device identifiers, advertising ID
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, photos access
  • No IP address or browsing history

Locally Stored Data

Only the following is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Game coins, high scores, unlocked levels (15 levels)
  • Shop items purchased (food shape, body pattern, full body, head character)
  • Game settings (sound on/off, vibration, trail)
  • Spin reward daily status

Ads and In-App Currency

The game has a "Watch Ad for Coins" feature which is currently simulated with a confirmation dialog. We do NOT use any third-party ad SDK such as Google AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, or Unity Ads in this version. No ad network collects your data. The coins are virtual and have no real monetary value.

If we integrate real ad SDKs in future updates, this policy will be updated and the ad networks' privacy policies will be linked here.

Third-Party Services

Snake Changer does NOT use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools.

Children's Privacy

Our game is rated for Everyone 3+ and does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The game is safe for children with no personal data collection.

Data Security

Since we do not collect any data on servers, there is no risk of data breach from our side. All local data remains on your device and you can clear it by clearing app data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
